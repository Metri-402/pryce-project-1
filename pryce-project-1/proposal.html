Portfolio Website Project Proposal

Website Purpose

The purpose of this website is to help me land a job in the IT field.

Target Audience

The target audience is for hiring managers looking for entry-to-mid level professionals skilled in technical proficiency, troubleshooting, and problem-solving.

Fundamental Portfolio Factors For Employment

12 elements of a career portfolio

  • Career Summary
  • Mission statement
  • Brief biography
  • Resume
  • Marketable skills
  • Professional accomplishment
  • Work samples
  • Awards
  • Transcripts, degrees, licenses and certifications
  • Professional development
  • Volunteer experience
  • Professional references & Testimonials

Fundamental Portfolio Factors For Website

6 elements for a professional portfolio website

  • Custom domain:establish professional credibility.
  • Mobile-first responsive design:recruiters often browse portfolios on their phones.
  • Clean Navigation:Portfolios targeting hiring managers or busy clients is not going to hunt through a mase of nested pages to find your best work.
  • Contact page with clear CTAs:portfolio exists to generate business inquiries, job interview requests, or collaboration opportunities.
  • Personal branding and about section:professionals in competitive fields where personality, values, and communication stlye matters as much as the work itself.
  • SSL security and regular maintenance:a 'not secure' warning in the browser bar is the fastest way to lose a potential client's trust
